ABOUT US

Building India's Most Trusted
Media Network

Founded with the vision of building a trustworthy and future-ready media organization, Dhaatri Media Broadcast Private Limited has grown into a multi- vertical media house combining journalism, public relations, and digital marketing under one roof.

Our team consists of experienced journalists, editors, digital strategists, content creators, designers, and media planners who understand both ground realities and digital-first audiences. We believe that strong stories, when told responsibly and strategically, have the power to create awareness, influence decisions, and drive growth.
